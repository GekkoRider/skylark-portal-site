We're delighted to have your family join us. This short guide covers everything you need to know before your child's first session — what happens next, how to get set up, and what a typical week looks like.

What happens next: our team will confirm your child's cohort, weekly session time, and tutor by email within one working day of joining. You'll also be able to see all of this any time on your dashboard.

Getting ready for the first session

  • A quiet space with a table, away from distractions
  • A laptop, tablet, or desktop computer with a working camera and microphone
  • A stable internet connection (a wired connection or good wifi signal works best)
  • Headphones, if possible — this helps your child hear their tutor clearly and reduces background noise for the group
  • A recent version of Chrome, Safari, or Edge (no software installation needed)

We recommend joining the classroom link a few minutes early the first time, just to check your camera and microphone are working.

What a typical week looks like

  1. One weekly live video session (two hours), in a small group of up to four pupils with the same tutor each week.
  2. A monthly digital activity pack — extra practice and activities matched to what your child is covering, available on your dashboard.
  3. A monthly progress update from your child's tutor, also on your dashboard, so you can see how they're getting on.
